faq:
  - question: How long does an Artlogic migration take?
    answer: Most gallery migrations run six to twelve weeks depending on the artist roster size, the state of the existing content, and how custom the design needs to be. I'll give a concrete timeline after the discovery call.
  - question: Will my Google rankings survive the migration?
    answer: Yes — provided redirects are mapped properly. I produce a complete 301 redirect map from your old URLs to the new Artlogic URLs and verify it post-launch in Search Console so you don't lose search equity.
  - question: Can you migrate from any platform?
    answer: I've migrated galleries from WordPress, Squarespace, Cargo, custom CMSs, and earlier Artlogic versions. If your current site has structured artist/artwork data we can usually bulk-import.
  - question: Do you handle the design too?
    answer: Yes — I'm a designer as well as a developer. Migrations can stay close to your existing brand or include a full redesign within Artlogic's template system.
